# Riftbreak content reference

Generated by `npm run content-docs` from `js/content/vocab.js` and the content itself. Do not edit by hand.
How to add content: [README.md](README.md).

## Stats

| Key | Label | On items | In statMods | Buffable | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `maxHp` | HP | yes | yes |  | Hit points. A unit at 0 HP is knocked out. |
| `maxMp` | MP | yes | yes |  | Mana for spells. Classes with no MP (Alchemist, Warlord lines) use skills that cost none. |
| `atk` | ATK | yes | yes | yes | Physical attack: the damage of physical skills. |
| `def` | DEF | yes | yes | yes | Physical defense. |
| `mat` | MAT | yes | yes | yes | Magic attack: the damage and healing of magic skills. |
| `mdf` | MDF | yes | yes | yes | Magic defense. |
| `spd` | SPD | yes | yes |  | Speed: how fast the unit's turn gauge (CT) fills. |
| `move` | Move | yes | yes |  | Tiles the unit can move in a turn. |
| `eva` | EVA | yes | yes | yes | Evasion: the chance, in percent, to dodge physical attacks (modified by facing). |
| `crit` | CRIT | yes | yes | yes | Critical hit chance in percent, on top of the base 5%. Only gear raises it. |
| `mpRegen` | MP/turn | yes |  |  | MP recovered at the start of each of the unit's turns: 3, plus gear. Units without MP recover none. |

`statMods` covers relics, event rewards (`memberStatMods`), enemy roster entries and summons (`summonMods`).

## Status effects

Any of these can be inflicted (`applyStatus`, `attackProc`, `attackerStatus`, `onMove.enemiesStatus`) or granted (`selfStatus`, relic `battleStart`). Cures remove the harmful ones.

| Key | Name | Kind | Effect | Inflicted by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `poison` | Poison | harmful | Loses about 6% of max HP at the start of each turn. | Toxic Spores, Plague Flask, Miasma, Venom Strike, Rending Maul, Cutthroat Stab |
| `slow` | Slow | harmful | The turn gauge fills at half speed. | Ice, Hex, Pinning Shot, Time Lapse, Grave Chill, Arcane Strike, Gravity Well, Tremor, Spirit Lash, Verdant Lash, Doom Hex, Wild Surge, Grasping Stone, Bite, Thorn Volley, Spore Burst, Venom Bite, Soul Drain, Bramble Lash, Crystal Slam, Spore Cloud |
| `silence` | Silence | harmful | Cannot use magic skills or magic reactions. | Mute, Spellbreaker |
| `sleep` | Sleep | harmful | Skips turns and cannot react. Any damage wakes the unit. | Dream Veil, Mass Sleep |
| `stun` | Stun | harmful | Loses its next turn and cannot react. | Calcify, Force Pulse, Holy Bind, Holy Chains, Haunting, Fate Seal, Savage Howl, Bulwark Bash, Shield Bash, Charge |
| `berserk` | Berserk | harmful | Can only attack the nearest enemy. | Primal Rage |
| `charm` | Charm | harmful | Fights for the other side and cannot react. | Bewilder, Bind Will |
| `frog` | Frog | harmful | Turned into a frog: ATK and MAT drop by 60% and DEF by 50%. | Hexform |
| `enfeebled` | Enfeeble | harmful | ATK is lowered by 5. | Enfeeble |
| `doom` | Doom | harmful | Falls when the countdown runs out, unless cured first. A boss loses a quarter of its max HP instead. | Death Sentence |
| `taunted` | Taunt | harmful | Must attack the unit that taunted it if it can. | Taunt, Challenge |
| `haste` | Haste | helpful | The turn gauge fills 50% faster. | Swift Grace, Quicksilver |
| `protect` | Protect | helpful | Takes 25% less physical damage. | Stone Aegis, Bulwark, Bone Ward, Shield Wall |
| `shell` | Shell | helpful | Takes 25% less magic damage. | Arcane Veil, Ward Circle |
| `regen` | Regen | helpful | Heals about 8% of max HP at the start of each turn. | Renewing Light, Stimulant |
| `reflect` | Reflect | helpful | Enemy spells bounce back at their caster (unless the spell ignores Reflect). | Mirror Ward |
| `reraise` | Reraise | helpful | The first knockout leaves the unit standing at 1 HP instead. | Soul Tether, Last Rites |
| `slipped` | Slipped | helpful | The next physical attack against the unit misses. | Temporal Slip, Smoke Bomb |
| `hidden` | Hidden | helpful | Enemies cannot see or target the unit until it acts. | Vanish |

### Engine states

Set by the engine, not by content (except `defend`, which a reaction can grant with `selfStatus`). Tactics scripts can check them.

| Key | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `defend` | Defend | Braced: takes less damage and dodges more until its next turn. |
| `charging` | Charging | Channeling a spell with a charge time. |
| `unconscious` | Unconscious | At 0 HP. Can be revived until its timer runs out. |
| `dead` | Dead | Out of the battle for good. |
