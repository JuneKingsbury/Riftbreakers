Accessibility
Battle
Each turn, the log names the tactics rules a character followed. Handy when you're writing your own tactics.
Equipment
When the party gets an item, it goes to whoever it helps most, as long as nothing they use gets worse. Whatever they took off is passed along the same way. Also on the Equipment screen during a run.
Save Data
Progress saves on this device automatically. Export a backup to keep it safe, or to move it to another device. It includes your renown, unlocks, skill plans and any run in progress.
Cloud Save (Google Drive)
Keep your progress in your own Google Drive and carry it between devices. The game can only see its own hidden folder, never your files.

Performance & Battery
Caps the game at 30 fps, draws at Low resolution, reduces effects and slows down while idle. Overrides the settings below while on.
Lower settings save battery, most of all on phones, tablets and high-refresh screens. Reduced effects halves particles and turns off screen shake and swaying grass. Low power when idle slows the game to 10 fps when nothing is moving and you haven't touched it for a couple of seconds.
