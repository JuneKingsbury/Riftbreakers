The service

Riftbreakers is a free single-player tactics game. It offers two optional online features:

  • Google Drive cloud save stores your progress in your own Google Drive so you can carry it between devices.
  • The online leaderboard lists high-scoring runs that players choose to submit, with a name of their choosing.

The game works fully without either, using local saves and manual export of save files.
